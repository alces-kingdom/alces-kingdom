Laws of Alces Kingdom

National rules founded on freedom, responsibility, and Christian values.

Article I — Religion and Beliefs

  1. Every citizen must identify as either Christian or non-religious.
  2. Christianity is the national religion of Alces Kingdom.
  3. Citizens may freely practice Christianity.
  4. Citizens may choose to have no religion.
  5. No citizen may be forced to believe in God.
  6. No citizen may be forced to attend church or participate in religious activities.
  7. The practice or promotion of other religions is not permitted under the nation's rules.
  8. Citizens must respect one another's personal dignity.
  9. Religious disagreements must be handled peacefully.
  10. Violence or threats based on religion or lack of religion are prohibited.

Article II — Freedom and Personal Rights

  1. Citizens have freedom of speech.
  2. Citizens may criticize the government peacefully.
  3. Citizens may express political opinions peacefully.
  4. Citizens have the right to privacy.
  5. Citizens may choose their own clothing and hobbies.
  6. Citizens may peacefully assemble.
  7. Citizens may form clubs and associations.
  8. Citizens have the right to a fair hearing if accused of breaking a rule.
  9. No official is above the laws of Alces Kingdom.
  10. Citizens may disagree without being punished for their opinions alone.

Article III — Crime and Public Safety

  1. Murder is prohibited.
  2. Attempted murder is prohibited.
  3. Assault is prohibited.
  4. Robbery is prohibited.
  5. Theft is prohibited.
  6. Threatening someone with violence is prohibited.
  7. Kidnapping is prohibited.
  8. Deliberately damaging another person's property is prohibited.
  9. Fraud and deliberate scams are prohibited.
  10. Arson is prohibited.
  11. Serious harassment and intimidation are prohibited.
  12. Reasonable self-defence is permitted when necessary to protect oneself or another person.
  13. Citizens must not use violence to settle personal disagreements.
  14. Deliberately making false accusations to harm another person is prohibited.
  15. Serious accusations should be investigated fairly.
  16. Punishments should reflect the seriousness of the offence.
  17. Minor disputes should normally be resolved peacefully.
  18. Nobody may use threats to force another citizen to obey them.
  19. Citizens must not deliberately endanger other people's lives.
  20. Everyone is entitled to fair treatment when accused of an offence.

Article IV — Economy and Private Property

  1. Private property is protected.
  2. Citizens may own businesses.
  3. Citizens may earn and save their own money.
  4. Citizens may trade goods and services freely.
  5. Citizens may choose their own lawful professions.
  6. Stealing money or property is prohibited.
  7. Fraudulent business practices are prohibited.
  8. Citizens may donate money voluntarily.
  9. Private property should not be taken without a legitimate legal basis.
  10. The government should avoid unnecessary interference in private businesses.
  11. Citizens may make voluntary agreements with one another.
  12. Business owners must treat customers honestly.
  13. Workers should be paid according to their agreed terms.
  14. The government should use public funds responsibly.
  15. Corruption and misuse of public money are prohibited.

Article V — Government and National Identity

  1. Alces Kingdom is an anti-communist micronation.
  2. The nation supports private ownership and individual responsibility.
  3. The government should have clearly defined powers.
  4. Government officials must act in the interests of the nation.
  5. Citizens may peacefully criticize government decisions.
  6. Political opposition is not a crime.
  7. No citizen may be forced to join a political party.
  8. No citizen must agree with every government policy.
  9. Government decisions should be communicated clearly.
  10. Public officials must not abuse their authority.
  11. Citizens may propose new laws.
  12. National rules should be reasonable and understandable.
  13. The government should protect citizens from violence and theft.
  14. National symbols should be treated respectfully.
  15. Alces Kingdom should seek peaceful relations with other micronations.

Article VI — Citizenship

  1. Citizenship is granted according to the official citizenship process.
  2. Citizens should respect the rights of others.
  3. Citizenship applications should be handled fairly.
  4. Citizens may leave Alces Kingdom whenever they wish.
  5. Participation in national activities is voluntary.
  6. New citizens should be welcomed respectfully.
  7. Citizens must not impersonate government officials.
  8. Citizenship may be suspended under established rules following serious misconduct.
  9. Revoking citizenship should follow a fair process.
  10. Citizens may suggest improvements to the nation.

Article VII — Online Conduct

  1. Citizens may express their opinions online.
  2. Credible threats of violence are prohibited.
  3. Doxxing intended to endanger or intimidate someone is prohibited.
  4. Scamming other citizens online is prohibited.
  5. Impersonation intended to commit fraud is prohibited.
  6. Citizens should respect other people's privacy.
  7. Political jokes and peaceful criticism are permitted.
  8. Social media disagreements should be handled peacefully.
  9. Nobody may falsely claim official authority.
  10. Serious online misconduct may be reviewed by the government.

Article VIII — Everyday Life

  1. Citizens may choose their own hobbies.
  2. Citizens may listen to the music they enjoy.
  3. Citizens may dress as they wish.
  4. Citizens may organize peaceful gatherings.
  5. Citizens may express their personalities freely.
  6. The government should not interfere unnecessarily in private life.
  7. Citizens should respect personal boundaries.
  8. Ordinary mistakes should not automatically result in punishment.
  9. Warnings may be used for minor first-time violations.
  10. Citizens are encouraged to help one another voluntarily.

Article IX — Fair Enforcement

  1. Laws apply to citizens and government officials alike.
  2. No one should be punished solely because an official dislikes them.
  3. Evidence should be considered before serious penalties are imposed.
  4. Accused citizens should have an opportunity to explain themselves.
  5. Citizens may appeal government decisions.
  6. Punishments should be proportionate to the offence.
  7. Minor conflicts should not become serious government matters without good reason.
  8. Rules should be applied consistently.
  9. New laws should be announced clearly.
  10. Changes to existing laws should follow the nation's established process.

Article X — Final Principles

  1. Freedom comes with responsibility.
  2. Violence is not an acceptable way to settle disputes.
  3. Murder, robbery, theft, assault, and threats of violence are prohibited.
  4. Private property and voluntary cooperation are important national principles.
  5. Christianity is the national religion, and citizens may also choose to be non-religious.
  6. No citizen is required to pretend to hold a religious belief.
  7. The government should not control every aspect of everyday life.
  8. Citizens should be able to live peacefully without unnecessary interference.
  9. The nation values independence, responsibility, order, and freedom.
  10. These laws serve as the official rules of Alces Kingdom.
